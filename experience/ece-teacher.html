01 Role and contribution职责与贡献

I worked in a culturally diverse ECE centre, applying Te Whāriki-informed practice with children, families, whānau and teaching colleagues.我曾在多元文化幼儿园工作，与儿童、家庭、whānau 及教学同事合作，践行以 Te Whāriki 为依据的幼儿教育。

  • Created language-rich learning environments with attention to oral language and early literacy.创设语言丰富的学习环境，关注口语与早期读写。
  • Supported belonging and wellbeing through relationships and daily routines.通过关系建立与日常活动支持儿童的归属感和身心健康。
  • Worked with families and colleagues to understand children’s interests and learning contexts.与家庭及同事共同理解儿童的兴趣与学习情境。

02 A foundation for practicum guidance实习指导的实践基础

Centre-based work informs the questions I ask about teaching decisions, relationships and participation when supporting student teachers. It helps me connect curriculum language with the realities of an ECE setting.幼儿园实践影响着我在支持师范生时如何追问教学决策、关系与参与，也帮助我将课程理念与幼儿教育场景中的实际工作联系起来。
