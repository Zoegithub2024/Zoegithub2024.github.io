01 Role and contribution职责与贡献

I mark assessments across seven ECE and education-related courses. My work involves reading learner submissions against course learning outcomes and assessment criteria, then explaining the judgement through constructive written feedback.我承担七门幼儿教育及教育相关课程的作业评估，依据课程学习成果与评分标准审阅学习者提交的证据，并通过建设性书面反馈说明评判依据。

  • Apply assessment criteria to the evidence in each submission.依据每份作业中的证据应用评分标准。
  • Provide feedback on academic writing, reflective practice and the use of course concepts.围绕学术写作、反思性实践和课程概念运用提供反馈。
  • Identify specific next steps so learners can understand what to strengthen.指出具体的改进方向，让学习者理解下一步需要加强什么。

02 What this brings to teacher education对教师教育的价值

Assessment work keeps me close to the difficulties adult learners encounter when connecting theory, evidence and professional practice. It informs how I explain expectations and structure academic guidance.评估工作让我持续接触成人学习者在连接理论、证据与专业实践时遇到的困难，也帮助我更清晰地解释课程要求并组织学术指导。
