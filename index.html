02 Teaching Philosophy教学理念

Every learner belongs.
Every voice matters.
Every step counts. 每一个学习者都有归属。
每一种声音都被听见。
每一步成长都算数。

🌱 Belonging🌱 归属感

Belonging归属感

Every learner brings a place, a language and a story. I build inclusive, Te Whāriki- and Te Tiriti-informed environments where children, families and adult learners feel they belong before they're asked to achieve.

每一位学习者都带着自己的成长背景、语言与故事而来。我致力于建立包容、体现 Te Whāriki 与 Te Tiriti 精神的学习环境，让儿童、家庭与成人学习者首先感受到归属，然后才被要求"达成"。
🗣️ Voice🗣️ 声音

Voice声音

Twenty years across New Zealand, China and the United Kingdom — and research into bilingual family literacy — have shown me that multilingual learners teach us as much as we teach them. I make space for every language and every voice.

二十年辗转新西兰、中国与英国的经历，以及对双语家庭读写的研究，让我深刻体会到：多语言学习者 教会我们的，并不比我们教给他们的少。我努力为每一种语言、每一种声音留出空间。
📈 Growth📈 成长

Growth成长

Feedback should move learners forward, not just judge them. I give clear, criteria-based, constructive feedback — and hold my own practice to the same standard: reflective, evidence-informed, always improving.

反馈应该推动学习者向前，而不只是评判他们。我提供清晰、基于评分标准、具建设性的反馈——也用 同样的标准要求自己：保持反思、以证据为依据、持续精进。

Doctoral Milestone博士研究里程碑

PhD Confirmation Completed完成博士 confirmation

18 August 20262026 年 8 月 18 日 · University of Canterbury坎特伯雷大学

I completed my PhD confirmation, presenting my proposed study, Bilingual Literacy Practices in Chinese–New Zealand Family Homes. The research explores how bilingual literacy takes shape in family life and, where relevant, how digital tools and texts are used within these practices.

The study brings together a sociocultural perspective, bilingualism and biliteracy, family literacy as social practice, and digital mediation. It centres on family members’ accounts of everyday literacy practices.

我完成了博士 confirmation，汇报了拟开展的研究《华人—新西兰家庭中的双语读写实践》。研究关注双语读写如何在家庭生活中展开，以及在相关情境中，数字工具与数字文本如何融入这些实践。

研究以社会文化视角为基础，结合双语与双语读写、作为社会实践的家庭读写，以及数字媒介等视角，重视家庭成员对日常读写实践的讲述。
Conference Presentation学术会议演讲

Recognising Bilingual Literacy in Family Life: What Might a Family Literacy Perspective Offer ECE?看见家庭生活中的双语读写：家庭读写视角能为幼儿教育带来什么？

19 September 20262026 年 9 月 19 日 · University of Waikato怀卡托大学
Early Childhood Futures: Sparking Joy in ECE

I presented a literature-led exploration of bilingual literacy in family life. Moving beyond which language is used, the talk considered what family members do with languages and texts, with whom, and for what purposes, within everyday relationships and routines.

I connected these ideas with early childhood education, inviting attention to families’ existing literacy resources and to what particular digital tools and texts may make possible or difficult.

我在会议上作了以文献与概念讨论为基础的演讲。从“家庭使用哪种语言”进一步追问：家人如何在日常关系与生活惯例中，与谁一起、为了什么目的使用语言和文本。

演讲将这些思考与幼儿教育联系起来，关注家庭已有的读写资源，并讨论特定数字工具与文本可能提供的机会和带来的限制。
Master’s Research硕士研究

Understanding the Educational Aspirations of Two Preschool Chinese Migrant Parents in One ECE Centre关于两位学前华人移民家长教育期望的研究：以一所幼儿教育中心为例

Master of Teaching Early Childhood Education, Distinction — Toi Ohomai Institute of Technology, 2021幼儿教育硕士（优异）— Toi Ohomai 理工学院，2021年

A qualitative case study exploring how two Chinese migrant parents in Tauranga understood their preschoolers' learning, and the language, cultural and power-imbalance barriers shaping their engagement with an ECE centre — with implications for culturally responsive home–centre partnership.

一项质性案例研究，探讨陶朗加两位华人移民家长如何理解学龄前子女的学习，以及语言、文化与权力 不对等因素如何影响他们与幼儿园的互动——为建立具文化回应性的家园合作关系提供启示。
